From lease PDF to evidence-backed field proposals

Upload a text-based PDF. The local server extracts its text; an optional local Ollama model proposes candidate fields; deterministic checks reject missing quotes or unsupported numbers. This is a research prototype, not accounting advice. It does not automatically approve or post entries.
